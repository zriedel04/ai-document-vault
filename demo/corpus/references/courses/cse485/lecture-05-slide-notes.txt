Lecture 5 Slide Notes: Design and Reviews
Slide 1: From requirements to design
Every design decision should trace back to at least one requirement.
Slide 2: Layers
- Presentation: what the user sees
- Logic: the rules of the application
- Data: where information is stored
Slide 3: Interfaces first
Agree on the interface between two components before either one is built. Write down inputs, outputs and errors.
Slide 4: Running a design review
A design review is a meeting where the team explains its design and a reviewer asks questions. Bring a diagram, a traceability table and a list of open risks.
The design reviews in this course are on October 30.
