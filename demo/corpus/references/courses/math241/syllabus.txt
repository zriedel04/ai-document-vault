MATH 241: Calculus II - Course Syllabus
Lakeshore Institute of Technology, Department of Mathematics. Fall 2026.
Course information
The course meets on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays from 9:00 AM to 9:50 AM. Instructor: Prof. Daniel Okafor, daniel.okafor@lit.example. His office hours are on Thursdays from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
Topics
- Techniques of integration
- Improper integrals and numerical integration
- Sequences and series
- Parametric curves and polar coordinates
Grading
- Homework: 20 percent
- Three unit exams: 15 percent each
- Final exam: 35 percent
Homework is submitted on the course portal every Monday. The lowest homework score is dropped.
Final exam
The final exam is on Tuesday, December 8, 2026 at 9:00 AM.
A scientific calculator is allowed. Graphing calculators are not.
Required textbook
Calculus: Early Transcendentals, 9th edition, by L. Brandt. The bookstore lists it at 112.50 dollars.
