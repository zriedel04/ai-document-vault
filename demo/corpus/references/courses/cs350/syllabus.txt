CS 350: Operating Systems - Course Syllabus
Lakeshore Institute of Technology, Department of Computer Science. Fall 2026.
Course information
The course meets on Mondays and Wednesdays from 11:00 AM to 12:15 PM. Instructor: Prof. Mei Tanaka, mei.tanaka@lit.example. Her office hours are on Tuesdays from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM.
Topics
- Processes and threads
- CPU scheduling
- Memory management and virtual memory
- File systems
Grading
- Programming projects: 40 percent
- Two in-class quizzes: 10 percent each
- Final exam: 40 percent
Final exam
The final exam is on Wednesday, December 9, 2026 at 1:00 PM.
One sheet of handwritten notes is allowed.
Projects
There are four programming projects. Each is done individually and submitted through the course portal.
